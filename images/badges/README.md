# Affiliation badges

Small derivatives of assets already included with this website:

- `georgia-tech.svg`: gold GT monogram from `../GTVertical_RGB.svg`.
- `ai4opt.svg`: institute emblem from `../AI4OPT-Logo-3.png`, cropped at x=550,
  y=73, width=220, height=276, resized to 96 pixels tall and clipped to omit the
  adjacent lettering. The original emblem pixels are embedded in the SVG.
- `amazon.svg`: the original letter a and smile paths from `../amazon-ar21.svg`,
  arranged as a compact icon.

Organization marks remain the property of their respective owners.
