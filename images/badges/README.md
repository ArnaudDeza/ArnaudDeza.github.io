# Affiliation badges

Small affiliation marks, served locally without third-party image requests:

- `georgia-tech.svg`: gold GT monogram from `../GTVertical_RGB.svg`.
- `ai4opt.png`: complete AI4OPT emblem and wordmark from the institute's
  official short-form logo, https://www.ai4opt.org/sites/default/files/images/AI4OPT-Logo-3.png
  (also present as `../AI4OPT-Logo-3.png`). Extracted at x=551, y=73,
  width=516, height=276 to separate it from the neighboring NSF seal, then
  reduced to 220 pixels wide. Its shapes, colors and proportions are preserved.
  A white backing keeps the black lettering legible in both themes.
- `amazon.png`: unmodified official About Amazon touch icon from
  https://www.aboutamazon.com/_next/static/media/apple-touch-icon.706b1b87.png
  (180 × 180 pixels). Uses Amazon's own smile artwork and proportions.

The AI4OPT and Amazon sources were retrieved November 1, 2026. Neither is
redrawn or assembled from pieces of a wordmark; both include enough source
pixels for high-density displays at their rendered sizes.

The previous `ai4opt.svg` and `amazon.svg` files remain available for cached
pages; the current author profile uses the PNG assets above.

Organization marks remain the property of their respective owners.
