Generative AI Summer-Camp

During my time at AI4OPT, I designed and created the GenAI summer-camp materials for the Seth Bonder Camp 2025. More specifically, I was in charge of creating the Level 4 GenAI course for Atlanta high school students that spans topics such as:

  • Generative AI with local LLMs using Ollama, pydantic and langchain.
  • Using pre-trained diffusion models to generate images.
  • Designing and building creative projects on agentic AI and tool use.
